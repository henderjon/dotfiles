FFMPEG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Cut a section out of a video
$ ffmpeg -i infile.mp4 -ss 00:00:47 -to 00:00:53 outfile.mp4

## Join images into a MOV video (time lapse style); framerate is how many images/frames per second
$ cat *.jpg | ffmpeg -f image2pipe -framerate 6 -i - -pix_fmt yuv420p -vf "pad=ceil(iw/2)*2:ceil(ih/2)*2" -c:v libx264 -crf 28 -preset veryslow out.mp4

## Convert video formats
$ ffmpeg -i "infile.3gp" -c:v copy -c:a copy "out.mov"
